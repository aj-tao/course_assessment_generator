	\begin{itemize}
		\item You will have \textbf{\printval{time}\ minutes} to complete the exam. There are \textbf{\printval{problemcount}\ problems}, each of which may have multiple parts. The entire exam totals up to \textbf{\printval{totalweight}\ points}.
		
		\item A graphing calculator is \emph{\printval{graphingcalculator}} for this exam.
		\item \textbf{This exam is closed-book.} You may not refer to external sources such as notes (including the course notes) and books, and you are not allowed to receive help from anyone else. Notecards are not allowed on the exam. 
		A formula sheet has been provided for you in the last page of the exam packet.
		
		\item \textbf{No work, no credit!} Unless otherwise indicated, every problem requires work to be shown; that is, you'll need to give a valid explanation or process along with your answer. 
		\item \textbf{Need space to show work?} Each problem page has remaining space for your work and solution; this includes the empty back page of the problem page. If there is work that you do not want to be counted for grading, cross it out with a large ``X." If you use an additional page for any problem, please write a note on the problem page (example: ``Go to page 4") telling the grader where to look, and label your work on those pages for the designated problem as necessary (example: to the left of your work for Problem 4(a), write ``4(a)").
		
		You may ask for extra scrap paper as provided by the Testing Center or Learning Support Services, and whatever scrap paper you have used will be collected and attached to the exam. Your work on scrap paper will \textbf{not} be counted for grading.
		\item All of your final answers must be in \textbf{exact form}, unless otherwise indicated. Examples of exact form answers are $\sqrt{2}/2$, $1+\log(2)$, $\pi/2-e^{-1}$, and so on. You should simplify your answers as much as possible; 
		for instance, $\f{\sqrt{27}}{2}-\sqrt{3}$ can be simplified to $\f{\sqrt{3}}{2}$.
		%for instance, $\sin\left(\f\pi3\right)-\sqrt{3}$ can be simplified to $-\f{\sqrt{3}}{2}$. 
		\emph{Do not round, unless a problem says so.}
		% Additional page policy from Testing Center?
	\end{itemize}
	\centering \Large{Good luck!}
